This incubation center is a multifunctional office building where innovative ideas are nurtured and aspiring entrepreneurs gain the skills they need before stepping into the professional world. The architectural design of the building is based on principles of flexibility and interaction. The open-plan layout supports individual work while also encouraging collaboration through shared working spaces.

The ground floor hosts multipurpose halls, seminar rooms, and a dynamic foyer area where events for entrepreneurs are organized. These spaces are separated by semi-transparent partitions, allowing them to be combined into larger areas for more extensive gatherings when needed. A central atrium ensures vertical transparency and allows natural light to penetrate deeply into the building, strengthening visual connections throughout the interior.

The upper floors include one-on-one consulting rooms and private office modules, designed with optimized sound insulation to prioritize privacy. Common areas are equipped with ergonomic furniture, and flexible modular systems allow the spaces to be easily adapted to users’ needs.

The building’s façade is clad in semi-translucent panels that filter daylight to create optimal indoor lighting. Passive solar control elements further enhance the energy efficiency of the exterior envelope. Sustainable design principles are embraced throughout the project, with materials chosen for their eco-friendliness—recyclable components, low-VOC paints, and energy-saving lighting systems contribute to the building's environmentally conscious identity.

This structure is not only a workplace but also an ecosystem where ideas, collaborations, and innovations are born. It offers entrepreneurs a physical space that provides every spatial and technical resource they need to realize their potential.
