Technoparks bring together companies from diverse sectors, fostering an environment where innovation in one field can catalyze advancements in others. This diversity enables a chain reaction of ideas, where a single concept can evolve and expand like a domino effect—transforming small thoughts into groundbreaking discoveries.

The building was designed as a continuation of the terraced typology commonly seen in existing technopark office structures. Based on the programmatic requirements, different user types were identified and grouped, resulting in a fragmented architectural form.

In order to reflect the identity of Çukurova University, transitional spaces between the units were emphasized. Columns inspired by tree trunks and a dual-layered dynamic canopy—representing the foliage—were introduced to evoke the atmosphere of a forest.
